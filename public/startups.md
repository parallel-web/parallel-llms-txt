## FAQs

* **Who is this for?**  
Early-stage teams building with live web data. If you're in one of our partner programs, you get that program's offer. Everyone else can apply with the [form](#apply).
* **Which programs do you partner with?**  
Y Combinator, a16z speedrun, Neo, Entrepreneurs First, Founders Inc and Null Fellows. If your program isn't listed, [we'd love to partner](mailto:startups@parallel.ai).
* **How do I redeem through my program?**  
Pick your program [above](#find-your-offer) and send your details via [the form](#apply).
* **I'm not in a program, or mine isn't listed. Can I still apply?**  
Yes! Fill out the [form](#apply) and tell us what you're building.
* **What do I need before I apply?**  
A Parallel [account](https://platform.parallel.ai/) and your org ID.
* **Where do I find my org ID?**  
In your [Organization settings](https://platform.parallel.ai/settings?tab=organization).
* **Do credits expire?**  
Six months from the issue date, with the exception of Founders Inc credits, which don't expire.
* **Can I combine offers?**  
One program offer per company.
* **What happens when my credits run out?**  
Move onto a paid plan from your billing page, or email [startups@parallel.ai](mailto:startups@parallel.ai) and we'll talk through options.

Running an accelerator or fund? Email [startups@parallel.ai](mailto:startups@parallel.ai) to offer Parallel to your founders.

Not a startup question? Reach us at [hello@parallel.ai](mailto:hello@parallel.ai).
