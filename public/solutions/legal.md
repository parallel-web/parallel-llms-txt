## FAQs

* **Does Parallel index international case law?**  
Yes. Parallel indexes court rulings and legislation across 60+ countries, spanning 300 domains and 500M+ pages — including jurisdictions general search engines miss — so legal AI products can ground answers in authoritative sources worldwide.
* **How does Parallel ensure legal citations are accurate and verifiable?**  
On Parallel's agentic APIs (Task, FindAll, Monitor, Responses), every output supports the [Basis framework](https://docs.parallel.ai/task-api/guides/access-research-basis): calibrated confidence scores, citations, source excerpts, and reasoning traces. Your team can check any claim against the underlying page before it reaches a lawyer or client.
* **Can legal AI tools built on Parallel replace Westlaw or Lexis+ search?**  
Parallel is the retrieval layer under legal AI products — not a direct end-user replacement for Westlaw or Lexis+. Platforms like Harvey use Parallel to reach international legal domains they couldn't build in-house, then present that research inside their own lawyer-facing experience.
* **What countries' legal databases does Parallel cover?**  
Coverage spans 60+ countries. Example jurisdictions already indexed include Brazil (state tribunal rulings), Argentina (regulatory codes), South Korea (committee resolutions), and Spain (tax authority Q&A), with more domains added continuously.
