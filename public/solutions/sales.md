## FAQs

* **What is account enrichment, and how does Parallel automate it?**  
Account enrichment turns a bare company or contact record into a complete profile. With Parallel's [Task API](https://docs.parallel.ai/task-api/task-quickstart), one call returns structured, cited fields — revenue, headcount, tech stack, funding, and strategic initiatives — ready for your CRM, with a confidence score and source on every field.
* **Can Parallel replace a data provider like ZoomInfo or Clearbit?**  
Parallel is live web research, not a static contact database. Instead of refreshing a fixed dataset, it researches the open web at query time and covers the long tail of companies, people, and signals that static providers miss. Many GTM teams use it alongside or instead of traditional enrichment vendors when freshness and coverage matter more than a prebuilt directory.
* **How does Parallel find and monitor buying signals?**  
The [Monitor API](https://docs.parallel.ai/monitor-api/monitor-quickstart) watches your target accounts continuously and alerts you when something noteworthy changes — new exec hires, funding rounds, product launches, job postings, and competitive moves — with deduping so you only hear about significant changes.
* **Does Parallel integrate with my CRM or GTM stack?**  
Yes. Parallel is API-first for custom pipelines, and ships ready integrations including Attio, [n8n](https://docs.parallel.ai/integrations/n8n), [Google Sheets](https://docs.parallel.ai/integrations/gsuite), and [Snowflake](https://docs.parallel.ai/data-integrations/snowflake). Drop enriched fields into your CRM, or wire Monitor webhooks into your existing GTM workflows.
