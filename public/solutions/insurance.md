* **Can Parallel automate like-kind-and-quality claims matching?**  
Yes. Parallel runs like-kind-and-quality matching, live pricing, and intake review line by line against your rules. High-confidence matches clear automatically; edge cases route to a human — so handling time drops and capacity shifts to work that needs judgment.
* **How does Parallel support P&C underwriting risk profiles?**
