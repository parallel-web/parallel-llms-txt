# Track holiday deals on flights and gifts with Parallel in ChatGPT

Starting today, you can ask ChatGPT to find and watch flights and products with Parallel. Instead of general price ranges, you get today's fares and prices, pulled from the live web.

## Fares as they are right now

Holiday fares change by the hour. With the Parallel plugin, ChatGPT checks current flight data for your exact dates and comes back with a real flight, its price and a link to book with the airline.

- "Find round-trip flights from SFO to JFK, December 20 to January 3, under $400."
- "What's the cheapest fare from Chicago to Lisbon for the week after Christmas?"

## Prices across millions of products

Ask for a gift and ChatGPT searches millions of products with Parallel, showing what each one costs today and where to buy it. Not ready to buy it yet? Ask Parallel to monitor it for you.

- "Find a Lodge cast iron skillet under $60 that ships before December 20. Only buy it if it’s below $60."
- "What's the best price on noise-cancelling headphones right now?"

## Install the Parallel plugin in ChatGPT

- Add the [Parallel plugin for ChatGPT](https://openai.com/business/plugins/parallel-search/).
- Sign in to Parallel, and add a credit card to get $5 in monthly credits.
- Ask ChatGPT to find a flight or product.

## Get live flight and product data for production agents

To bring flight and product search to your own product, check out the [Parallel API docs](https://docs.parallel.ai). The [Flights API](https://docs.parallel.ai/flight-search/flight-search-quickstart) and [Shopping API](https://docs.parallel.ai/shopping-search/shopping-search-quickstart) are currently in early access.
