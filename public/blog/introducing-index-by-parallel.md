# Introducing Index by Parallel

Today we're launching [Index](http://index.parallel.ai), a platform that helps content owners understand how AI agents use their work and earn compensation tied to the value they contribute.

## How it works

Any site owner can enter their domain at [index.parallel.ai](http://index.parallel.ai) to see how Parallel's agents are using their content right now: which queries it's answering, how often it's referenced, and how unique its contribution is to the work the agent performed.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/5hzduz3y/production/c98ec5865038738615b947269d404a69ab749e35-3042x1848.png)

Compensation is calculated by estimating each source's Shapley value, its marginal contribution to the work an agent performs at the moment of inference. Content that's uniquely valuable, hard to replace, or used in high-value agent work earns more.

## Why now

AI agents will use the web 1000x more than humans, but the old model of the web doesn’t work for them.

The old model was: publishers let search engines in, and they sent readers back. But AI agents don’t see ads or consume content the way humans do. They read the page, answer the question, and the human never arrives. Seventy-nine percent of major news sites now block at least one AI crawler. The licensing deals that do exist are flat fees, negotiated bilaterally, available only to content owners with the scale to get a meeting. Index introduces a new incentive-aligned economic model that spans the open web, benefiting both big and small players alike.

## What's next

We'll expand Index across more categories of content and open self-serve participation so any publisher can join. The platform is also open to AI agents built outside Parallel, so the market grows as agent demand grows.
