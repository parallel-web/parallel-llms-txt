GC AI uses Parallel’s Search API to bring authoritative legal sources from the live web into cited answers for more than 2,200 in-house legal teams.

- **Trusted authoritative legal sources at a global scale.** Parallel’s Search API finds authoritative legal sources across jurisdictions, including hard-to-find sources.
- **Better answers for in-house teams.** More accurate, well-supported answers are part of why companies choose GC AI.
- **86.8% on the **[**In-House Legal Bench**](https://gc.ai/blog/in-house-legal-bench-evaluating-ai-assistants-for-in-house-legal-work)**.** GC AI led ChatGPT-5.5 (79.8%) across 100 in-house legal tasks, with its largest lead on research-intensive tasks.

## About GC AI 

[GC AI](https://gc.ai/) is the leading AI platform for in-house legal teams. Co-founded by [Cecilia Ziniti](https://gc.ai/company/cecilia-ziniti), a three-time general counsel, the platform serves more than 2,200 corporate legal departments including teams from Vercel, Arc’teryx, Wayfair, and TIME. GC AI research combines real-time access to authoritative legal sources with AI-powered analysis, helping legal professionals find answers, assess risks, and verify conclusions with confidence. Every insight is supported by transparent, traceable citations back to the original source material.

## The importance of live web search to in-house teams

GC AI serves companies, so the research has to get both the legal side and the business side right. In-house teams need information beyond primary sources, including recent regulatory updates and agency guidance that can be hard to find. Supported by Parallel’s Search API, GC AI’s web search can find those hard-to-find sources and return them in one answer a lawyer can verify.

For lawyers, reliable sources are important for making trusted decisions. GC AI designs and runs its own evals on research quality, depth, breadth and accuracy. On top of web search results, GC AI’s own proprietary research layer, built by lawyers, thinks like a lawyer and puts authoritative resources first, like official government sites. On the [In-House Legal Bench](https://gc.ai/blog/in-house-legal-bench-evaluating-ai-assistants-for-in-house-legal-work), GC AI scored 86.8% across 100 in-house legal tasks, ahead of ChatGPT-5.5 at 79.8%, with the largest advantages appearing in research-intensive tasks.

Parallel’s Search API ranks pages by relevance and returns current pages and excerpts. The Extract API pulls full additional context from the page’s text. To keep pace with new developments, GC AI users create Automations (GC AI’s scheduled agents) to monitor the practice areas they care about and flag new developments, without having to search manually.
