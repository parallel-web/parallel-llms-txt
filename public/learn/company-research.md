## What is company research?

Company research means collecting and making sense of public and private information about a company to answer a specific question. That question might be whether to sell to it, invest in it, work for it, or compete with it. You start with the decision, then gather the facts it needs.

Sales teams, investors, job seekers, recruiters, and partnership leads all run this work. Each person asks a different question, so each one weighs different facts about the same business.

The work turns scattered facts into a profile you can act on. Those facts include firmographics, financials, news, hiring signals, and the company’s tech stack.

On their own they read as noise. Pulled together, they show who a company is and where it’s headed.

Most research starts with [AI web search](https://parallel.ai/products/search) across authoritative web sources, then narrows to the details that answer your question.

## Key characteristics

Good research shares a few traits, whatever the decision behind it.

- **Question driven:** Every effort starts with a decision it needs to support.
- **Multiple sources:** It pulls from public filings, company websites, news, review sites, and directories.
- **Freshness matters:** Demand for fresh, structured company data keeps rising, and [data enrichment market growth](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/data-enrichment-solutions-global-market-report) is projected to reach $5.13 billion by 2030 at a 12.2% CAGR, according to The Business Research Company (2026).
- **Accuracy has a cost:** Gartner research from 2020 found the [cost of poor data quality](https://www.gartner.com/en/data-analytics/topics/data-quality) reaches at least $12.9 million per year on average.
- **Verifiable:** Good research traces each fact to its source, which supports efforts to [discover companies at scale](https://parallel.ai/products/findall) that match specific criteria.

## Example

Picture a sales rep researching a target account before outreach. They pull the founding year, headcount, funding history, and recent news into one briefing.

An investor runs the same play before a first meeting. They add ownership structure, revenue signals, and competitor moves, so the conversation starts from evidence.

The time cost is real. The [Salesforce State of Sales report](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/) found that the average seller spends 40% of their week selling. Tasks like data entry and internal meetings consume the rest.

## FAQ

**What are the main sources for company research?** The main sources are public filings such as SEC EDGAR for U.S. public companies, the company’s own website, news coverage, review sites, and business directories.

**How is company research different from market research?** It focuses on one specific business. Market research studies a whole market, industry, or customer segment.

**Can company research be automated?** Yes, AI research tools can search the web, read sources, and return structured company profiles with citations in minutes.
